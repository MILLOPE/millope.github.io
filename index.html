About

I'm Shumin Meng, a Master's student in Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore (NUS). I received my bachelor's degree from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in June 2025.

My interests lie in embodied artificial intelligence, vision-language-action models, and agentic robotic systems.

I'm particularly interested in developing robotic systems capable of long-horizon task execution through perception, reasoning, planning, and closed-loop interaction.
